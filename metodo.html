The method

← screener · signals follow-up

The screener applies Stan Weinstein's stage analysis to the whole stock universe (US + Euronext + XETRA). The idea: buy when a stock breaks out of an accumulation base and enters Stage 2 (uptrend), confirmed by volume and relative strength.

The 4 stages

  Stage 1 · Base    Stage 2 · Advance    Stage 3 · Top    Stage 4 · Decline  

  1. Stage 1 — Base: after a decline, price moves sideways around a flat MA30 (accumulation).
  2. Stage 2 — Advance: breakout above the base, MA30 rising → this is where you buy.
  3. Stage 3 — Top: the advance stalls, the MA30 flattens (distribution).
  4. Stage 4 — Decline: breakdown, MA30 falling → stay out (or short).

How a signal is born

At the weekly close, a candidate is full if: there was a mature base after a decline, the close breaks the resistance of the base and is above the MA30 (not falling), volume surges, relative strength (Mansfield) is positive and rising, and market + sector are in Stage 2. "Near" candidates fail one or two of these filters: the screener shows them for your judgment.

Structural parameters (active values)

MA3030 weeksthe 30-week moving average that defines the 4 stages: above and rising = bull, below and falling = bear.
MA slopeover 4 w · flat if |slope| < 0.005tells a rising/falling MA from a sideways one.
Min base≥ 12 weeksthe accumulation (Stage 1) must be mature: months, not days.
Base depth≤ 30%a valid base is tight and orderly, not a roller-coaster.
Breakout volume≥ 2.0× the 4-week averagea real breakout is confirmed by a volume surge.
Mansfield RS≥ 0 and rising for 4 w (52-week window)relative strength vs market: the stock must be stronger than the index.
Base volumecontraction ≤ 1.0 · or OBV accumulation ≥ 0.1in the base volume dries up (dry-up) or shows accumulation, then explodes.
Anti-newsrejected if the close is > 25% above resistancea huge news gap isn't an orderly break: it often retraces.
Requirement Athe base must follow a decline (Stage 4)accumulation AFTER a decline, not a pause inside a trend.
Market filterStage 2first screen: buy only if the index is in an uptrend.
Sector filterStage 2 (SPDR ETFs, US)second screen: market → sector → stock.
Stopstructural pivot, 2% buffer, on the weekly CLOSEbelow the last relative low; evaluated on the close (weekly method).
Executionnext_openbuy at the open of the week AFTER the signal (no look-ahead).

An example

Real example: SAX.XETRA · signal on 2026-07-31 · entry 39.64 · stop 31.85. Colored background = stages; dashed line = base resistance; green triangle = ENTRY; red line = STOP; below, volume with the 4-week average.
Honest about the limits. The method does not beat the market outright (it stays too much in cash: it's market timing). But the selection, measured against a "random twin" at equal exposure and same stops, adds modest but positive value (~59th percentile). The real value is discretion: a few stocks chosen with judgment, not 20,000 mechanically → scanner + human judgment.
